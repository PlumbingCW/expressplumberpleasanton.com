Emergency sewer line repair in Pleasanton is for the line that has stopped being a clog problem and become a pipe problem. If cleaning gives you a few weeks and then the backups return, something in the pipe is broken, offset or full of roots. We send a licensed plumber, camera the lateral, and quote a repair in plain terms. Call (510) 544-1962 at any hour.

Until we arrive, use as little water as possible and do not pour chemicals down the drain. Chemicals add risk for the plumber and do nothing for a broken pipe.

Sewage and gas safety

What to do first: sewage and gas

Raw sewage carries bacteria, so keep children and pets out of any flooded area, wear gloves and boots if you must enter, and wash well afterward. If a backup comes with a rotten egg smell, leave the house and call 911 from outside, then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000.

Signs your sewer line is failing, not just clogged

Symptoms that point to damage

  • Backups that return after every cleaning, often within weeks.
  • Slow drains in the lowest fixtures first, with gurgling at the toilet.
  • A sewage smell in the yard or a patch of lawn that stays wet and green.
  • Cracks in a driveway or slab above the line, or a sunken strip of ground.
  • A camera image showing roots, offset joints, a flat spot or broken wall.

A localized crack with root intrusion is one of the most common findings, and it is often a candidate for a spot repair instead of a full replacement. The camera tells us which, which is why we do not quote a repair before looking.

A liner is not magic. It cannot straighten a sagging section, and a collapsed pipe has to be dug. We tell you which of the four options matches your camera findings, even when that means the cheaper repair is the better call.

What local conditions do to a sewer lateral

Clay and silt soils shrink in drought and swell in wet years. After the four-year drought, Pleasanton's Gates neighborhood saw cracked foundations and driveways, and city staff accepted a Zone 7 study linking the damage to those soils drying out. Ground that moves like that can shift a buried pipe just enough to offset a joint, and an offset joint catches paper and roots.

Mature trees add the other half of the story. Roots follow moisture into any joint that weeps. Older neighborhoods near Downtown Pleasanton often have the clay or cast iron pipe and the big trees, while newer areas such as Callippe Preserve have plastic pipe that is less prone to root entry.

Permits and who is responsible

In Pleasanton the City maintains trunk sewers, and the Public Works page assigns side sewers and laterals to the property owner under Section 15.32.100. In nearby Livermore the city publishes the same split and says a plumbing permit is needed for lateral repair or replacement. Permit rules vary, so ask the Pleasanton Building and Safety Division what applies at your address.

Where a repair crosses a sidewalk or street, an encroachment permit and a call to 811 before digging are standard. We handle the permit steps as part of the job when they apply.

Insurance and warranties

Many homeowner policies do not cover the repair of a sewer line that failed from age or roots, though some offer an endorsement. Ask your agent, and keep the camera report we give you. It documents the damage for any claim or for a future buyer.
