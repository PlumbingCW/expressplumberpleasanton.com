Emergency Hydro Jetting in Pleasanton, CA

When snaking only opens a narrow channel, high-pressure water jetting cleans the full pipe wall. We scope the line first, then jet it clean in Pleasanton and the Tri-Valley.

Emergency hydro jetting in Pleasanton is the answer when a drain or sewer line backs up, gets snaked, and backs up again. A jetter pushes water through a hose and spins it out of a nozzle at high pressure, scouring the whole inside of the pipe instead of punching one hole through the clog. If sewage is rising now, call (510) 544-1962 and keep every fixture off until we arrive.

While you wait, do not run the washing machine or flush. Stopping the water going in is the single most useful thing you can do, because a full lateral has nowhere else to send it.

Signs a snaked drain needs jetting instead

People rarely search for hydro jetting by name. They search for the symptom, so these are the situations that lead here.

The pattern that points to jetting

  • The clog came back within a few weeks of a cable visit.
  • The snake got stuck or only pulled out a small plug.
  • Slow drains in several fixtures even though nothing flushes back.
  • A drain smell that returns after cleaning, which suggests buildup on the pipe wall.
  • A line with a known history of roots, grease or mineral scale.

How the jetting visit works

What happens on site

  1. We locate the cleanout or an access point and confirm where the line leaves the house.
  2. A camera goes in first so we can see roots, scale, a sag or a crack before any pressure is used.
  3. The jetter runs from the cleanout toward the street, then back, with the nozzle changed to suit grease, scale or roots.
  4. A second camera pass confirms the pipe wall is clean and shows anything that needs repair.
  5. You get a plain description of what we saw and whether a follow-up service interval makes sense.

Jetting compared with snaking and rodding

Which tool for which clog.
ToolWhat it does wellWhere it falls short
Cable snakeBreaks up a soft clog or hair mat quicklyLeaves a film on the pipe wall that rebuilds.
Root cutter bladesCuts roots out of the lineRoots regrow unless the entry joint is repaired.
Hydro jetterCleans grease, sludge and scale off the entire pipe wallNot for crushed, offset or collapsed pipe.
Camera onlyShows exactly what is wrongDoes not clear anything.

Jetting is not safe for every pipe. Cracked clay, collapsed cast iron and joints that have shifted can be damaged by pressure, which is why the camera pass comes first and why we tell you when a spot repair is the better call.

Jetting a Pleasanton lateral

The pipe from your house to the street connection is yours to maintain in Pleasanton, according to the city's Public Works customer service page. Lateral lines under older neighborhoods near Downtown Pleasanton can be clay or cast iron, and roots find their joints. In newer subdivisions the plastic lines clog from grease and wipes instead.

Rain matters too. Pleasanton averages roughly 14 inches of rain a year, and a root-filled lateral can surcharge during a wet winter week when the ground around it is saturated. If a backup repeats in storm season, mention that to the dispatcher.

Restaurants, offices and multi-unit buildings

Kitchen drains in Hacienda Business Park offices and downtown restaurants collect fats and starch faster than a house ever does. A scheduled jetting pass keeps a grease line open and prevents the weekend backup that stops a dinner service.
