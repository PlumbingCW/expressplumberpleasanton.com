Plumber in Downtown Pleasanton, CA

Downtown Pleasanton is the historic center of the city, between Bernal Avenue and Del Valle Parkway. Homes here are among the oldest in Pleasanton, and some were built in the late 1800s, so a plumber has to look at each house on its own.

We serve the residential streets around Main Street and the shops, restaurants and offices along it. Below is what public records say about the area, followed by the work we do.

Older homes call for inspection, not assumptions

A house that is more than a century old has probably been updated in several rounds. A kitchen may have new pipe behind the cabinets while a bathroom wall still holds something from an earlier decade.

We do not guess what is inside a wall from the year a house was built. A camera, a pressure check or a short exploratory opening tells us what we are working with, and then we give you the repair choices and the price.

What we fix in downtown houses

  • Drain cleaning for kitchens, tubs and floor drains that clog repeatedly.
  • Leak detection when a wall or floor stays damp or the water bill creeps up.
  • Sewer inspection to see the condition of the line running to the street.
  • Repiping estimates when a house has had repeated supply line leaks.
  • Toilet repair for tanks that run, leak at the base or do not flush well.

Main Street shops, restaurants and offices

Businesses near Main Street have different needs from houses. A restaurant has kitchen drains and grease, a salon has sinks and a water heater, and an office has restrooms that cannot stay out of service.

Our commercial plumbing page explains the work we do for businesses. If you run a shop downtown, tell us the building and whether you are the owner or a tenant, since that affects who approves repairs.

Before we arrive

If water is running in an older downtown building

  1. Close the main valve. In older houses the valve may be stiff, so turn it slowly and firmly.
  2. If it will not close, tell us right away, because the valve itself may need replacing.
  3. Move belongings away from the wet area and photograph the damage.
  4. Call (510) 544-1962 and tell us the address, whether it is a house or a business, and where the water is.

Homes in Ruby Hill are very different, and we serve both ends of the city. Our main Pleasanton page has the full service list.
