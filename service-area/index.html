Pleasanton Express Plumbing is based on Hacienda Drive in Pleasanton, Alameda County, and dispatches along I-580, I-680 and Stanley Boulevard. Distances and drive times below are estimates, and traffic changes them, so the dispatcher gives you a window when you call (510) 544-1962.

Pleasanton itself uses ZIP codes 94566 and 94588. Its neighborhoods include Downtown Pleasanton, Hacienda Business Park, Ruby Hill and Callippe Preserve, and our service pages describe what each tends to need.

How the towns connect to Pleasanton

Livermore sits east of Pleasanton along I-580, so the drive is a straight run on one freeway. Dublin is to the north, where I-580 and I-680 meet. San Ramon and Danville follow along I-680 to the north-northwest, and Danville is the farthest of the four.

Distance matters less than the hour and the traffic. A call at 2 a.m. reaches Danville faster than a call at 5 p.m. on a weekday, when commuters fill I-680. That is why the dispatcher gives you a window instead of a promise, and why calling early in the day gives you the best chance of a same-day appointment.

Each town has its own plumbing profile. Livermore has older downtown streets and large growth-era tracts, Dublin has newer neighborhoods next to 1960s tracts, San Ramon mixes homes with Bishop Ranch offices, and Danville has mid-century streets and gated Blackhawk. The town pages explain what that means for pipes, water heaters and sewers.
