Emergency Water Heater Repair in Pleasanton, CA

A cold shower is a nuisance. A leaking tank is an emergency. We repair gas, electric and heat pump water heaters in Pleasanton and tell you honestly when repair is not worth the money.

Emergency water heater repair in Pleasanton covers two very different calls. One is a heater that simply stopped making hot water, which is annoying and often a cheap part. The other is a tank that is leaking onto the floor, which can ruin a garage slab or a laundry room wall in a few hours. Our 24/7 line at (510) 544-1962 treats the second one as urgent.

If water is pooling, turn off the cold supply to the heater at the valve on top, switch off the power or set a gas unit to pilot, and open a hot tap in the house to relieve pressure. Then call.

A leaking water heater: shut it off first

The first five minutes

  1. Find the cold water shutoff on the pipe above the tank and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Switch off the breaker for an electric heater, or turn a gas control to pilot or off.
  3. Open a hot tap upstairs to let the pressure drop.
  4. Run a hose from the drain valve at the bottom to a floor drain if the tank is still leaking, or place towels and a bucket.
  5. Move stored items away from the base and photograph the leak for your insurer.

Where the water comes from decides the repair. A drip from the temperature and pressure relief valve or a loose fitting can be fixed in place. Water seeping from the tank body or its seam means the steel has failed, and the honest answer is replacement, not repair.

No hot water: what we check

Failed heaters tend to fall into a handful of causes. On a gas unit, the thermocouple, pilot assembly or gas control valve are the usual suspects. On an electric tank, a burned heating element or a tripped high-limit switch is common. On a heat pump unit, filters, condensate drainage and the control board need attention.

A heater that makes some hot water and then runs cold quickly often has a broken dip tube or a lower element that failed. A heater that makes lukewarm water may be set too low, or sediment may be insulating the burner or element from the water.

Gas, electric and heat pump water heaters

Gas and electric tanks are the familiar kinds, and most repairs are quick. A hybrid heat pump heater is more efficient but has more parts, including a compressor, a fan and a condensate line, and its repair list is different. We service all three. If the manufacturer's warranty is still active on a newer unit, we explain what the warranty covers before you pay for a repair.

Pleasanton water and your tank

The City of Pleasanton's 2024 water quality report lists average hardness of 86 mg/L for Zone 7 treated surface water and 309 mg/L for groundwater. On the USGS scale that spans moderately hard to very hard, depending on the blend that reaches your tap. Hard water leaves scale on elements and burners, which is why heaters in the Tri-Valley benefit from an annual flush.

Test your tap or read your water retailer's annual report to see where your address falls. If the number is high, a softener helps the heater last longer. Our water softener and filtration page covers that decision.

Water damage and insurance

A sudden tank failure that floods a room is often covered by a homeowner policy, while a slow leak that went unnoticed may not be. Photograph everything, turn the water off, and call your insurer. We can provide a written note about the failure.
