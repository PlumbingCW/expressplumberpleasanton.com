Emergency Faucet Repair in Pleasanton, CA

A drip can waste gallons a day, and a failed shutoff or a spraying fixture can flood a kitchen. We repair and replace faucets, shower valves and hose bibs in Pleasanton.

Emergency faucet repair in Pleasanton ranges from a drip you can ignore for a day to a fixture that is spraying water onto cabinets. Most faucet problems come from a worn cartridge, washer or O-ring, or from mineral scale. If water is actively spraying, close the supply valves under the sink and call (510) 544-1962.

Why faucets drip, spray or run slowly

Faucet symptoms and causes.
SymptomLikely causeTypical fix
Steady drip from the spoutWorn cartridge, washer or seatReplace the cartridge or washer.
Leak at the handleWorn O-ring or loose packing nutReplace the O-ring or tighten.
Water at the base of the faucetFailed gasket or loose mountingReplace seals and re-secure.
Low flow or uneven sprayScale in the aerator or cartridgeClean or replace the aerator.
Hot and cold mix wronglyFailed mixing cartridgeReplace the cartridge.
Handle will not turn offStripped stem or failed valveReplace the stem or fixture.

Pleasanton's water is hard in places. The City of Pleasanton's 2024 water quality report lists average hardness of 86 mg/L for Zone 7 treated surface water and 309 mg/L for groundwater, so mineral buildup on aerators and cartridges is common. A vinegar soak helps a clogged aerator, and our water softener page covers a longer-term fix.

Kitchen, bathroom and shower fixtures

Kitchen faucets work harder than any other fixture, and pull-down sprayers, hoses and cartridges all wear. We repair them when parts are available and replace the faucet when the finish is failing or the body is corroded.

Shower valves hide behind tile. A leaking handle or temperature that will not hold usually means a failed cartridge, and we can replace that without removing the tile. If the valve body itself leaks, access is needed through the wall, and we explain the options first.

Outdoor faucets and hose bibs

A hose bib that drips at the handle needs a new washer or packing. One that leaks inside the wall is a bigger problem, because water can reach framing for weeks. A wet wall below an outside spigot should be checked quickly.

Disconnect hoses when not in use, since a hose left on holds pressure on the faucet and a cold night can crack a stem. Frost-proof bibs are a good upgrade where they fit.

Pull-down, touchless and filtered faucets

Modern kitchen faucets add hoses, spray heads, solenoids and batteries. A pull-down hose that will not retract usually has a stuck weight or a kinked line. A touchless model that stops responding often needs fresh batteries or a cleaned sensor before anything else.

Faucets with a built-in filter or a hot water dispenser have extra lines under the sink, and we trace them before ordering parts so you pay for the right fix. A fixture beside the faucet may need attention too, and toilet repair covers the bathroom side.

Repair or replace the faucet

Decision guide.
SituationBetter choice
Newer faucet, single worn partRepair.
Corroded body or flaking finishReplace.
Discontinued model with no partsReplace.
Remodel or new sinkReplace with a matching fixture.
Repeated repairs within a yearReplace.

We can install a faucet you bought or supply one. Larger fixture and layout projects are handled under plumbing installation. The City of Pleasanton's bathroom handout lists 1.2 gallons per minute for bathroom faucets and 1.8 for kitchen faucets, so check the flow rating before you buy.

Shutoff valves under the sink

Every fixture should have its own shutoff valve. If the valves under your sink are stuck, weeping or missing, we replace them during the visit. That small job means the next faucet repair takes minutes, and it gives you a way to stop a leak without shutting off the whole house.
