Emergency water heater replacement in Pleasanton usually starts with a puddle. The tank has rusted through, the seam has opened, or the heater is old enough that a repair makes no sense. Shut off the cold water and power, open a hot tap to relieve pressure, and call (510) 544-1962. We replace the unit, haul the old one away and restore hot water, same day when the schedule allows.

When a failed tank means replace, not repair

Signs the heater has to go

  • Water seeping from the tank body, seam or the base of the jacket.
  • Rust-colored hot water or flakes from the tank interior.
  • A heater past 12 years old with a repair that costs close to a new unit.
  • Repeated element, thermocouple or valve failures within a year.
  • Loud rumbling or popping from sediment baked onto the tank floor.

A leaking steel tank cannot be patched. If your heater is leaking from the tank itself, replacement is the only fix, and the sooner the water is shut off, the less damage you have to repair.

The Bay Area air rule that affects gas heaters

The Bay Area Air Quality Management District's Rule 9-6 sets zero-NOx standards for residential gas water heaters, and the standards apply when a unit is replaced. The district posted on its Building Appliances page that its board adopted amendments on November 4, 2026 to move the compliance date to January 1, 2028, with exemptions for qualifying low-income owners and for properties with major electrical or space limits.

The rule does not require replacing a working heater. If you are buying a new gas heater today, ask the installer which model is allowed at the time of installation, and check baaqmd.gov for the current text.

What happens on replacement day

The usual sequence

  1. We confirm the size and fuel type and walk through the venting, gas or electrical needs.
  2. The tank is drained, disconnected and carried out.
  3. The new heater is set, connected and fitted with new flex lines, a relief valve and a discharge pipe.
  4. Gas connections are leak tested and electric connections are checked before we fire up.
  5. You get a walkthrough of the shutoff, the temperature setting and the maintenance schedule.

The City of Pleasanton's water heater handout says a plumbing permit is required to install, remove, relocate or replace a water heater. The handout is dated January 2020 and cites the 2019 California Plumbing Code, so confirm the current edition with the Building and Safety Division. We pull the permit and meet the inspector.

The same handout calls for two seismic straps, an expansion tank where the water system is closed and a relief valve drain ending 6 to 24 inches above grade. For emergency work, the city's permit handout says the application is due within the next business day. We handle that filing and point out anything missing from your setup.

Sizing a replacement for a Pleasanton household

A 40-gallon tank suits a one- or two-bathroom home with a small family. Larger households and homes with soaking tubs often need 50 gallons or more, and a family with high morning demand may prefer a tankless unit. The first-hour rating on the label matters more than the gallon count.

Homes near Ruby Hill and Callippe Preserve are typically larger and may have two heaters or a recirculation loop. We check what you have before sizing.

A note on insurance

A sudden tank failure that damages a room may be covered, while the heater itself usually is not. Keep the old tank until the adjuster has seen it if you plan to file a claim, and photograph the area before cleanup.
