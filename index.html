Emergency plumber in Pleasanton

An emergency plumber who answers at night and names the price first

An emergency plumber in Pleasanton comes out at night, on weekends and on holidays when a pipe, a drain or a water heater fails and cannot wait. Our 24/7 emergency line at (510) 544-1962 is staffed around the clock, and the dispatcher stays on the phone until you know what to shut off.

We handle the calls that cannot wait: a burst pipe, a sewer backup, a flooding water heater, an overflowing toilet and a gas appliance that needs a safe shutoff. We also do the planned work that keeps those calls from happening, such as camera inspections, water heater installs and repiping.

You hear a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows, and a visit after hours costs more than a daytime visit because of the hour. If a repair is not worth the money, we say so and show you the alternative.

Local knowledge

Pleasanton neighborhoods and what they mean for your plumbing

Downtown Pleasanton has the city's oldest streets and homes, so we inspect before we recommend a repair. Callippe Preserve lies south of Happy Valley Road, around a golf course that opened in 2005. Ruby Hill is a gated community in East Pleasanton, where large lots often mean longer pipe runs between the meter and the house.

Hacienda Business Park, near the Dublin/Pleasanton BART station, is a large office area in north Pleasanton, and its tenants call about restrooms, kitchens and water heaters. Our commercial plumbing page explains what we handle for offices, restaurants and retail units.

Pleasanton's water is supplied by Zone 7 and disinfected with chloramine, according to the City of Pleasanton's annual water quality report. The same report says hardness runs from 100 to 450 mg/L, or 6 to 26 grains per gallon, depending on the source. That is why scale is a standing topic in our water heater repair and water softener visits.

Before we arrive

If you have a burst pipe or an active leak, do these four things

STEP 1

Close the main valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise at the house or the meter, then open a low faucet to drain the line.

STEP 2

Cut power near water

Switch off the water heater and the breakers for wet rooms, only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

STEP 3

Clear the area

Move rugs, electronics and valuables, and photograph the damage for your insurer.

STEP 4

Call the 24/7 line

Call (510) 544-1962 and tell us what you see. We will confirm an arrival window.
