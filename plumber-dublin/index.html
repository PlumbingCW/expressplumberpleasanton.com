Emergency plumbing in Dublin is a short trip for us. The city borders Pleasanton on the north side, and our 24/7 emergency line at (510) 544-1962 sends a plumber about five miles up I-680 or along Dublin Boulevard, usually in around ten minutes. We answer nights, weekends and holidays.

Dublin uses a single ZIP code, 94568. The neighborhoods inside it are very different from one another, so here is how we think about each.

West Dublin: the 1960s tracts

The first housing tracts in Dublin were built in West Dublin in the 1960s, and growth continued steadily after that. Homes from that era are now more than fifty years old. The original supply pipe, the drain lines under the slab or floor and the first water heater generation are all well past their design life.

Typical calls from older Dublin streets include slow main drains, tree-root intrusion and pinhole leaks. A drain cleaning visit with a camera shows whether roots or a belly in the pipe is the cause. Our sewer line repair page explains what comes next if the pipe itself is damaged.

East Dublin and the newer subdivisions

Dublin's population roughly tripled between 1990 and 2020, and most of that growth went east. Wallis Ranch opened its first phase in 2016 with about 816 homes, and Tassajara Hills added roughly 370 detached houses. Homes this new rarely have old pipe, but they often have tankless heaters, recirculation pumps and long runs from the heater to the farthest bathroom.

Calls from those streets tend to be about water heaters, error codes, low flow at the far fixture and fittings that were never quite tight. See tankless water heater service for flushing and diagnosis.

Apartments and townhomes near BART

Dublin has built transit-oriented housing near its two BART stations, Dublin/Pleasanton and West Dublin/Pleasanton, including rental communities such as Avalon Dublin Station and Valor Crossing. In multi-unit buildings, a single failed valve or a shared drain can affect several homes at once.

Property managers get a written note from us listing what failed, where it is and what we did. That helps when a landlord, a tenant and an insurer need the same facts. For shared drains and building-wide problems, see commercial plumbing.

Before we arrive in Dublin

STEP 1

Stop the flow

Close the main shutoff, or the building valve in a multi-unit property, and tell neighbors below if water may travel through the ceiling.

STEP 2

Protect the floor

Move rugs and furniture and put down towels, since water spreads fast on laminate and tile.

STEP 3

Shut down the heater

If the water heater is leaking, turn off its cold supply and set the gas control to pilot or cut its breaker.

STEP 4

Call us

Call (510) 544-1962 with your address and unit number. We will confirm an arrival window before the truck leaves.

Dublin water and where the supply comes from

Dublin San Ramon Services District provides water service to Dublin. Zone 7 Water Agency supplies Dublin and lists DSRSD as one of its four retailers. Because the City of Pleasanton's 2024 report lists Zone 7 treated surface water at an average of 86 mg/L of hardness, that figure is a reasonable starting point, but the DSRSD annual report is the one to read for your tap.

Hard water is mostly an issue for heater life and fixture scale. If you see crust on aerators or a rumbling tank, see water softener and filtration.

Roads, parks and how we reach you

Dublin Boulevard runs east and west just north of I-580, and Foothill Road and Dublin Canyon Road reach the west side.
