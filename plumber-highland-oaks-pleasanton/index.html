A neighborhood next to open hills

Homes at the edge of open land are often reached by narrower streets and sit on lots with slopes, mature trees and hose bibs on all sides of the house. Those are general observations about such settings, not documented facts about every Highland Oaks lot.

For a plumber, the practical points are access, drainage and trees. A line near a mature tree can pick up root intrusion over time, and a camera look is the way to find out without digging. We tell you what the camera shows before recommending any repair.

California requires water heaters to be braced against earthquake movement under Health and Safety Code section 19211. In a Bay Area hillside neighborhood that rule matters, so the straps on your tank are worth a look whenever we service it.

Common calls for Highland Oaks homes

  • Leak detection for wet patches, a high water bill or a meter that moves when everything is off.
  • Burst pipe repair when a line fails inside a wall, ceiling or crawl space.
  • Water heater repair for pilot problems, leaks and slow recovery.
  • Sewer inspection when a main drain backs up more than once.
  • Faucet repair for drips, loose handles and low flow.

A house that was built in the late 1960s may have been remodeled since. Kitchens and bathrooms are the usual places for updated fixtures, and the supply and drain lines behind them can differ from room to room.
