How to Repair a Leaky Outdoor Faucet: A Pleasanton, CA Homeowner Guide

A dripping hose bib wastes water and, if the leak is inside the wall, can damage framing. Most outside faucet leaks are simple to fix, and this guide shows which ones.

Call (510) 544-1962
By the Pleasanton Express Plumbing teamUpdated November 13, 20267 min read

Most outdoor faucet leaks come from a worn washer, a loose packing nut or a damaged stem. You can often fix them in under an hour with a wrench, a screwdriver and a few parts. If water leaks inside the wall or from the pipe behind the faucet, the repair is different, and a plumber should look at it.

Find where the water appears before you take anything apart. A drip from the spout points to the washer or seat. A leak around the handle points to the packing. Water inside the house or a wet wall below the faucet points to the pipe.

Where the leak shows up and what it means.
Where you see waterLikely causeFix
Dripping from the spout when closedWorn washer or damaged seatReplace the washer or the stem.
Leaking around the handle when openWorn packing or a loose packing nutTighten the nut or replace the packing.
Leaking at the hose connectionWorn hose washerReplace the rubber washer in the hose coupling.
Water behind the faucet or inside the wallCracked pipe or a failed connectionShut off and call a plumber.
Water sprays from a vacuum breaker capNormal drainage or a worn breakerCheck for hose left attached, replace the breaker.

What you need before you start

Tools and parts to gather

  • An adjustable wrench and a screwdriver.
  • A replacement washer kit or stem for your faucet brand, which you can match by bringing the old part to a hardware store.
  • Plumber's tape and a rag.
  • A flashlight to find the indoor shutoff for the faucet.
  • A bucket for water that drains from the pipe.

Replace the washer and packing

The basic repair

  1. Close the indoor shutoff valve for the outside faucet, or the main water valve if you cannot find it. Open the faucet to drain the line.
  2. Use a wrench to loosen the packing nut behind the handle, then unscrew the handle and remove the stem.
  3. Look at the washer at the end of the stem. If it is cracked, flattened or hardened, replace it. Check the packing and swap it if it is dry or crumbling.
  4. Reassemble, open the shutoff slowly and test for drips at the spout and the handle.
  5. If it still leaks, the seat inside the faucet body may be worn. That is the point to call a plumber or replace the faucet.

Disconnect hoses when you are not using them. A hose left attached holds pressure against the faucet, and a vacuum breaker needs to drain to work. Many dripping bibs are simply hoses left on all week.

Pleasanton billing, meters and a leaking hose bib

The City of Pleasanton's leak adjustment form lists leaking faucets and leaking hose bib connections among the leaks that are within a customer's control. Those leaks are not eligible for a bill adjustment, so a drip you can see is worth fixing right away.

To check whether the leak is bigger than a drip, the city's meter guide says to turn all the water off at the property and watch the flow rate screen for one to two minutes. A reading of 0.00 GPM means no leak, and any movement, such as 0.05 GPM, means a possible leak.

Source type: City of Pleasanton utility billing leak adjustment request form and City of Pleasanton water conservation guide to reading your digital meter.

Frost-proof bibs and long-term upgrades

Pleasanton rarely sees hard freezes, but cold nights do happen, and an exposed bib on a north wall with a hose attached is the first thing to crack. A frost-proof sillcock places the shutoff seat inside the warm part of the wall, so water does not sit in the exposed pipe.

If your faucet is old and the finish is pitted, replacing it often costs less than repeated repairs. A plumber can also add a shutoff valve indoors, which makes the next repair faster and safer.

When to call a plumber instead

Call if water appears inside the house near the faucet, if a wall or ceiling below it is stained, if the stem is seized and will not turn, or if the packing nut spins without tightening. A hard-to-reach bib behind a deck or a corner of a stucco wall is another reason to call.

Our faucet repair team handles hose bibs, shower valves and kitchen fixtures. If you suspect a leak you cannot see, leak detection finds it with listening gear and pressure tests.

Choosing a replacement bib and what it costs

If the bib is too worn to repair, you have three common choices. A standard sillcock is the cheapest and works where freezing is rare. An anti-siphon bib has a vacuum breaker that stops dirty water from flowing back into the house, and a frost-proof bib moves the shutoff seat deep inside the wall.

A frost-proof bib must match the thickness of the wall, so measure from the outside face to the inside of the framing before you buy. Price depends on the type, on whether the pipe behind it is copper, PEX or galvanized and on whether the plumber can reach the connection from a crawlspace, a garage or only from inside a finished wall.

Ask for a shutoff valve indoors, close to the faucet, while the wall is open. It costs little and gives you a quick way to stop a leak next time without closing the whole house.
