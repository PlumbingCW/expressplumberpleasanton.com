A safe reset for a jammed disposal

Try this before you call

  1. Switch off the disposal at the wall switch and unplug it under the sink if you can reach the cord.
  2. Look in the sink with a flashlight. Never put your hand inside, and use tongs or pliers to pull out visible objects.
  3. Insert the hex key that came with the unit into the center socket at the bottom, and work it back and forth to free the flywheel.
  4. Press the red reset button at the bottom of the unit.
  5. Restore power, run cold water and test.

If the unit hums but does not turn, the blade is likely jammed. If it does not hum at all, the reset button has tripped or the unit has lost power. If it leaks, the issue is a seal or the sink flange.

Repair or replace the disposal

A jam or a loose connection is a quick repair. A leaking housing or a motor that burns out is the end of the unit. If your disposal is over ten years old and has started to leak or trip, replacement is usually the sensible move.

We match horsepower to the household. A half horsepower unit suits light use, and three quarters to one horsepower handles a busy kitchen and tougher scraps.

What should and should not go down

Habits that keep a disposal working

  • Run cold water before, during and after use for about fifteen seconds.
  • Cut large scraps into small pieces and feed them in gradually.
  • Keep grease, oil and fat out of the drain, even in liquid form.
  • Avoid fibrous items such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves, plus coffee grounds in volume.
  • Never put bones, pits, glass, metal or non-food items in the unit.

Grease is the biggest threat to the drain after the disposal. It cools and coats the pipe, and a line that backs up repeatedly may need hydro jetting.

Disposals and the kitchen drain together

The disposal and the trap beneath it form one system. A new disposal bolted to a partly clogged drain will still back up. We check the drain and the dishwasher connection during every disposal visit, because the dishwasher drains through the same system.

If the sink drains slowly even with the disposal off, see drain cleaning. If you are adding a disposal where there was none, the plumbing installation page covers the electrical and drain work.
