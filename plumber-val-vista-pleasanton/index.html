Why drains are the Val Vista call we expect most

Single-story houses of similar size often share a layout, with the kitchen, bathrooms and laundry on one level and a main drain running under the slab or crawl space. When that drain slows down, several fixtures show symptoms together.

That is the pattern we look for first. If a kitchen sink and a tub back up in the same hour, the cause is probably in the main line, not in either fixture, and sewer line cleaning is the right first step.

The 1971 sewer moratorium is a historical detail about the city system at the time. It does not tell us anything about the condition of the sewer lateral serving your house today.

Work that changes pipe, fixtures or a water heater can require a city permit. Ask how permits are handled for your job before work begins, and keep the paperwork for your records and for any future sale of the house.

If a drain backs up in your Val Vista home

First steps while you wait

  • Stop running water and do not flush, because the water has nowhere to go.
  • Check whether the backup is at one fixture or several, and tell us when you call.
  • Keep children and pets away from standing water in the bathroom or laundry.
  • Do not pour drain chemicals into a line that may need a camera look or a cleaning machine.
